object_MedicalProbeMachine                    Medical Probe Machine
buildtoolbar_popup_obj_MedicalProbeMachine    Runs the Strip Search room it stands in. Every prisoner who steps into the room is probed, which hurts a little, and a guard is called to search them. Crooked guards and crates are searched too, and anyone carrying a crate sets it down to be searched. Nobody unsearched leaves the room.
object_RoomScanner                            Room Scanner
buildtoolbar_popup_obj_RoomScanner            Stands on the floor of a Strip Search room and catches crooked guards. A crooked guard who comes to search in the room searches as an honest one would, so a bribe buys nothing, and comes out of the job exposed.
room_stripsearch                              Strip Search
buildtoolbar_popup_room_stripsearch           Anyone who steps in is searched by a guard before they can leave. Zone it across a corridor or a doorway and put a Medical Probe Machine and a Room Scanner in it. It needs no doors of its own. Keep guards close by: with none free, people wait.

tooltip_MedicalProbeMachine_Holding           Holding people until a guard has searched them.
