object_StripSearchScanner                    Strip Search Scanner
buildtoolbar_popup_obj_StripSearchScanner    Runs a Strip Search room. Build it in a walled room with a Strip Search Entrance and a Strip Search Exit, or use the quick builds. It lets people in through one door, shuts them in, calls guards to search every prisoner, crooked guard and crate inside, and only then opens the other door. Nothing crosses the room without a search.

buildtoolbar_popup_obj_StripSearchDoorIn     The way into a Strip Search room. The room's scanner holds it: open while people are coming in, shut while they are searched, and never open together with the exit.
object_StripSearchDoorOut                    Strip Search Exit
buildtoolbar_popup_obj_StripSearchDoorOut    The way out of a Strip Search room. The room's scanner holds it shut until everyone inside has been searched, so nothing gets past the room unsearched.
