Name                 "Contraband Checkpoint Scanner"
Author               Knight
Description          "A room that searches every prisoner who walks through it. Zone a Contraband Checkpoint across a corridor and its scanner holds each prisoner there until a guard has searched them. Crates get searched on the way through too, and a crooked guard who does the searching gets exposed.\r\n\r\n[h2]How to use it[/h2]\r\n[olist]\r\n[*]Subscribe, turn the mod on in Extras > Mods, and load your prison.\r\n[*]Pick a choke point: a corridor, a doorway or the way into a cell block. Between the workshop or the kitchen and the cells is a good one.\r\n[*]Open the rooms menu and zone a Contraband Checkpoint across it, at least two tiles by two. It needs no doors of its own and no particular walls or floor.\r\n[*]Place a Contraband Scanner ($500) anywhere inside. It runs the room.\r\n[*]Place a Guard Scanner ($800) anywhere inside. It catches crooked guards.\r\n[*]Station or patrol guards nearby. They do the searching, and with none free, people wait.\r\n[/olist]\r\nThe scanner's light is green with nobody waiting and red while it is holding someone.\r\n\r\n[h2]How it works[/h2]\r\n[list]\r\n[*]The scanner reads a prisoner as they step in, which does them no harm, and calls a guard over to search them.\r\n[*]It has crates searched too, on the floor or in someone's arms. Whoever is carrying one sets it down in the room first, and a guard escorting a prisoner lets go of them for the search. The game picks the crate or the escort back up afterwards.\r\n[*]It has any guard the game knows to be crooked searched as well.\r\n[*]Nobody unsearched leaves. The room has no door to hold shut, so the scanner puts anyone who steps out early back where they last stood inside.\r\n[*]Nobody waits for ever. The scanner lets a person go after ninety seconds without a search.\r\n[*]Other staff, visitors and dogs walk straight through.\r\n[/list]\r\n\r\n[h2]The Guard Scanner[/h2]\r\nIn the base game a bribed guard skips the confiscation. With a Guard Scanner in the room, a crooked guard who comes to search does the job honestly and comes out exposed, with the game's own marker over them, for you to deal with. Without one the checkpoint still runs, but a bribed guard's search there can miss.\r\n\r\n[h2]What changes in your game[/h2]\r\n[list]\r\n[*]A guard confiscates whatever they find at the checkpoint, on the spot.\r\n[*]People wait in the room until a guard is free, so traffic through it slows down when your guards are busy.\r\n[/list]\r\n\r\n[h2]What it cannot do[/h2]\r\nThese are the game's own rules, and no room changes them.\r\n[list]\r\n[*]A guard whose needs are neglected skips the confiscation about half the time, so keep your guards' needs met.\r\n[*]Contraband thrown over a wall or dug under one never passes through the room.\r\n[*]The game will not search the same person twice within thirty seconds, so someone searched that recently walks straight through.\r\n[/list]\r\n\r\n[h2]Conflicts[/h2]\r\nNone known. The mod adds one room and two objects and changes nothing the game already has."
